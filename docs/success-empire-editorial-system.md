# Success Empire Daily Correspondence

This is the operating standard for the two daily Success Empire publications on
`sitanimafi.com`. The website owns the complete writing. Email and Telegram only
carry a concise, useful teaser and a route back to the published page.

## The relationship promise

Every edition should feel as though Sitani wrote it to one intelligent family
member he respects. It may teach, challenge, or admit uncertainty, but it must
never posture. The reader should finish with one clearer idea, one practical
next step, and a stronger sense of who Sitani is becoming.

The voice is:

- Specific before sweeping.
- Warm before impressive.
- Honest about unfinished work.
- Direct without becoming preachy.
- Ambitious without macho shorthand.
- Useful without turning into a listicle.
- Personal without exposing private clients, systems, credentials, or people.

## Morning principle

Sender: `Success Empire <newsletter@sitanimafi.com>`

The morning edition is a 700–1,200 word principle with exactly these sections:

1. **The Principle** — state one memorable principle in plain language.
2. **Why It Matters** — connect it to a recognizable human tension or true
   experience.
3. **How to Apply It** — give concrete actions a reader can take today.
4. **Reflection for Today** — close with one useful question or small practice.

The principle must stand on its own. It cannot depend on a trend, fabricated
story, famous-person attribution, or motivational cliché.

## Afternoon letter

Sender: `Sitani Mafi <CEO@sitanimafi.com>`

The afternoon edition is a 700–1,400 word letter grounded in the actual day. It
starts with a natural salutation, uses three to five short sections, closes like
a note to family, and is signed `Sitani`.

The letter should:

- Begin with a real moment, decision, frustration, lesson, or update from the
  day.
- Explain what changed in Sitani's thinking.
- Make the lesson useful to the reader without turning the reader into an
  audience segment.
- Admit uncertainty when the work is still in progress.
- Link the morning principle at the bottom of the website post.

Operational context may be abstracted, but invented specifics are forbidden.
Client names, confidential metrics, internal code, credentials, and unpublished
business details never belong in the public letter.

## Editorial scorecard

Every generated draft is scored out of 100 and must earn at least 85.

| Dimension | Points | Passing evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Authenticity | 20 | A concrete human observation; no invented authority or false certainty |
| Clarity | 15 | One central idea, plain language, and a coherent progression |
| Applicability | 25 | Specific actions, questions, or practices the reader can use |
| Emotional resonance | 15 | Stakes are felt without melodrama or manipulation |
| Relationship | 15 | Written to one person; generous, direct, and genuinely connective |
| Originality | 10 | A distinct framing that does not repeat recent titles or canned advice |

In addition to the score, any hard failure rejects the draft:

- Raw internal labels, production notes, markdown scaffolding, or system names.
- “As an AI,” generic content-calendar language, or unsupported claims.
- Hustle, domination, alpha, warrior, conquest, or shame-based motivation.
- A quotation attributed to someone without a verified source.
- A made-up personal event, client result, conversation, number, or promise.
- A URL inside the article body.
- A sales pitch disguised as a principle.
- Copy substantially duplicating a recent Success Empire edition.

## Teaser-only conversion contract

The full content is published and verified on `sitanimafi.com` before any
notification is sent.

Email contains only:

- Publication identity.
- Title.
- One short teaser derived from the deck.
- One primary button to the full website post.
- A minimal footer.

Telegram contains only:

- Publication identity.
- Title.
- One short teaser.
- An inline button to the full website post.
- For the afternoon letter, a second inline button to the morning principle.

No section body, full principle, full journal entry, production receipt, source
status, internal metadata, or raw markdown may be sent through either channel.

## Delivery and recovery rules

- Morning is generated once per local calendar day.
- Afternoon receives a deterministic randomized time between 3:05 PM and
  5:55 PM Mountain Time so retries keep the same target.
- Published content is immutable. A retry only repairs missing delivery.
- Email and Telegram use stable idempotency keys.
- Provider rejection, suppression lookup failure, website verification failure,
  or Telegram permission failure is a visible failure, never a false success.
- A monitor repairs missing work after the afternoon window and reports any
  incomplete chain.
- The Utah Main Street daily-events workflow is outside this system and must not
  be changed.
